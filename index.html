Research

Re-implementations of canonical problems with experiment tables. Not claimed as new attacks, new detectors, or accepted workshop papers.

I

Game-theoretic IDS sampling

2026 · student manuscript · GameSec-style evaluation

Seeded re-implementation of the sampling-budget IDS game (Kodialam & Lakshman, INFOCOM 2003). Under a tight budget (Bs/MF ≈ 0.30) on a 3×3 grid with decoy links, min-cut allocation reached detection 0.300 versus 0.161 for uniform (87% relative gain). The course derivation is cited separately.

Repository Report

II

Simulated SPA of textbook RSA-CRT

2026 · student manuscript · CHES/COSADE-style evaluation

Simulated simple power analysis of square-and-multiply RSA-CRT (not ChipWhisperer, not OpenSSL). A single noiseless opcode trace recovered dp and factored 1024-bit n on 100% of seeded trials. A Montgomery ladder and dummy multiply made that distinguisher fail (0% exact dp). Message blinding still leaked dp; exponent blinding hid dp but the recovered dp′ still factored n.

Repository Report

III

Constrained adversarial examples against a flow NIDS

2026 · student manuscript · AISec/RAID-style evaluation

Feature-space FGSM/PGD on a CIC-IDS2017 Wednesday DoS subsample. Not a new detector and not a packet replay. Unconstrained PGD reached 82% ASR at scaled L∞ ε = 0.5; a padding/delay mutability mask lowered ASR to 46%. Constrained examples still transferred to a random forest at 61%. PGD adversarial training cut constrained ASR to 8%.
